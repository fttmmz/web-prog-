A note left at the front desk

Welcome, reader.

Four books from one series were borrowed from this library and never returned. They are hidden somewhere inside. Your job is to find them and put them back.

  1. Explore the rooms Click, hover over and type on anything that looks out of place.
  2. Find the secrets Each one gives back a lost book and opens a new room. The small shelf at the top shows how many books you have.
  3. Use the Main Hall It is the map of the library. Go back to it to enter the rooms you have opened.
  4. Return the series Once you have all four books, put them back on the shelf on your Checkout Card to open the last room.

If you are lost, ask the librarian. The “Ask the librarian” button is at the top of every page.

Start right here, at the front desk.
